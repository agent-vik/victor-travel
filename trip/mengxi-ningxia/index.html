Western Inner Mongolia & Ningxia Self-Drive

巴彦淖尔—乌海—石嘴山—银川 6 天 5 晚小众自驾：沙海、峡谷与黄河沿线。

Six-day self-drive across western Inner Mongolia and northern Ningxia.

  • 6天5晚
  • 6 days / 5 nights

行程

时间：10月xx日-10月xx日（6天5晚）

交通
飞机到巴彦淖尔，租车通勤，银川飞机回杭州。
住宿
巴彦淖尔2晚（世奥酒店临河维多利摩尔城店），乌海1晚（格兰云天酒店高铁站店），石嘴山1晚（冉升酒店万达广场店），银川1晚（美丽豪酒店北京东路店）。
D1D2D3D4D5D6
D1

出发

萧山机场(12:20) - 天吉泰机场(18:45)

落地后机场取车，开到市区，不安排游玩。

D2

D2

景点：乌兰布和沙海湖(3小时) - 黄河国家湿地公园(1小时)

  • 乌兰布和沙海湖门票

酒店出发去乌兰布和沙海湖。去了看情况，好玩就玩全天，不行还可以早点出来，赶到黄河国家湿地公园看日落。当晚还住巴彦淖尔。

D3

D3

景点：鸡鹿塞(0.5小时) - 阿贵庙(0.5小时) - 西部梦幻峡谷(3小时)

  • 西部梦幻峡谷门票

酒店出发，依次游玩这几个景点，时间如果不够就略过阿贵庙。西部梦幻峡谷出来不能太晚，因为要再开2个小时到乌海。当晚住乌海市。

D4

D4

景点：乌海湖(6小时)

乌海湖游玩一整天。不能太晚出来，还要再开一个半小时。当晚住石嘴山市。

D5

D5

景点：沙湖(6小时)

  • 沙湖门票

沙湖景区，好玩就玩一天，或者提早出来去银川，提前把贺兰山岩画玩掉。当晚住银川。

D6

返程

河东国际机场(15:00) - 萧山机场(18:00)

景点：贺兰山岩画(2小时)

  • 贺兰山岩画门票

贺兰山岩画如果没去，来得及就去一下。然后去机场还车，返程。

检查清单

行李清单可使用Excel表格管理。

小提示

  • 由于跨市移动，每天都要买些干粮备着，以防堵车影响吃饭。
  • 10月初气温白天20~25度，晚上0~5度

Itinerary

Dates: Oct xx–Oct xx (6 days, 5 nights)

Transport
Fly into Bayannur, drive between cities, fly from Yinchuan back to Hangzhou.
Accommodation
Bayannur 2 nights (Shiao Hotel Linhe Victory Mall), Wuhai 1 night (Grand Cloud Hotel High-Speed Rail Station), Shizuishan 1 night (Ransheng Hotel Wanda Plaza), Yinchuan 1 night (Meilihao Hotel Beijing East Road).
D1D2D3D4D5D6
D1

Departure

Xiaoshan Airport (12:20) - Tianjitai Airport (18:45)

Pick up the car at the airport after landing, drive into town; no sightseeing.

D2

D2

Sights: Ulan Buh Desert Sea Lake (3 hours) - Yellow River National Wetland Park (1 hour)

  • Ulan Buh Desert Sea Lake tickets

Hotel → Ulan Buh Desert Sea Lake. Play it by ear—if it’s great, stay all day; if not, leave early and catch sunset at Yellow River National Wetland Park. Night in Bayannur again.

D3

D3

Sights: Jilusa (0.5 hours) - Agui Temple (0.5 hours) - Western Dream Canyon (3 hours)

  • Western Dream Canyon tickets

From the hotel, visit these in order; if short on time, skip Agui Temple. Don’t leave Western Dream Canyon too late—another 2-hour drive to Wuhai. Night in Wuhai.

D4

D4

Sights: Wuhai Lake (6 hours)

Full day at Wuhai Lake. Don’t leave too late—another 1.5-hour drive. Night in Shizuishan.

D5

D5

Sights: Sand Lake (6 hours)

  • Sand Lake tickets

Sand Lake scenic area—stay all day if it’s fun, or leave early for Yinchuan and knock out Helan Mountain Rock Art ahead of schedule. Night in Yinchuan.

D6

Return

Hedong International Airport (15:00) - Xiaoshan Airport (18:00)

Sights: Helan Mountain Rock Art (2 hours)

  • Helan Mountain Rock Art tickets

If you haven’t done Helan Mountain Rock Art yet, go if time allows. Then return the car at the airport and fly home.

Tips

  • Because you’re moving between cities every day, buy some dry snacks each day in case traffic delays meals.
  • Early October: daytime about 20–25°C, nights 0–5°C

Yinchuan
