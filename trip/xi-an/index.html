D4

（5月xx日）

  • 西安博物院

白天先去西安博物院和小雁塔，下午转到大雁塔片区，晚饭后看大唐不夜城夜景。中间留休息和吃饭缓冲。

D5

（5月xx日）

景点：西安城墙(3小时) - 广仁寺(1小时) - 化觉巷清真大寺(1小时)

  • 西安城墙
  • 化觉巷清真大寺

老城核心日，城墙作为主景点，从南门/永宁门上城租车，往西骑到西北城角或玉祥门附近下城，接广仁寺；若西北角不能还车，则在西门/安定门下城后前往广仁寺。周六尽量不要太晚登城，若租不到合适自行车，就改为步行一段或乘城墙观光车。化觉巷清真大寺作为机动项，有余力可去。

小提示

  • 5月中下旬西安白天通常25-30°C、夜间约14-18°C，降水中等（约每月8-10个雨日）；注意防晒，带薄外套和轻便雨具。
  • 周末人流集中在城墙、曲江和夜游区域，D5城墙尽量不要太晚登城。
  • 大唐芙蓉园不要只白天去，夜景是核心体验；装造日按还衣时间倒推离园。

D1

(May xx) Departure

  • Seat selection
  • Check-in
  • Xi'an Bell & Drum Towers

Hangzhou Xiaoshan (10:45) – Xi'an Xianyang (13:25)

Sights: Xi'an Bell Tower (1 hr) – Xi'an Drum Tower (1 hr) – Muslim Quarter (2 hr)

After arrival, check in at the hotel and rest. In the afternoon, explore the Bell & Drum Tower area and Muslim Quarter; dinner in the Muslim Quarter.

D2

(May xx)

  • Emperor Qinshihuang's Mausoleum Site Museum
  • Huaqing Palace

Sights: Emperor Qinshihuang's Mausoleum Site Museum (4 hr) – Huaqing Palace (2 hr) – Yongxing Fang (1 hr)

Full day on the Lintong east line: morning trip to the Terracotta Warriors, prioritizing a guided tour and core galleries, then Huaqing Palace for Tang imperial bath sites and Xi'an Incident areas. Skip *Song of Everlasting Sorrow*; return to the city for dinner and strolling at Yongxing Fang in the evening.

D3

(May xx)

  • Hanfu costume & makeup
  • Tang Paradise

Sights: Tang Paradise (5 hr)

Costume-and-photo day at Tang Paradise. Keep the morning light with no rigid schedule—allow time for changing, makeup, and transit. Enter in the afternoon for a slow stroll and photos; wait for lights and night views; pace your departure around the costume return deadline.

D4

(May xx)

  • Xi'an Museum
  • Giant Wild Goose Pagoda

Sights: Xi'an Museum (3 hr) – Giant Wild Goose Pagoda (2 hr) – Grand Tang Mall (2 hr)

Morning at Xi'an Museum and Small Wild Goose Pagoda; afternoon at the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda area; Grand Tang Mall night views after dinner. Buffer for rest and meals in between.

D5

(May xx)

  • Xi'an City Wall
  • Great Mosque of Xi'an (Huajue Lane)

Sights: Xi'an City Wall (3 hr) – Guangren Temple (1 hr) – Great Mosque of Xi'an (1 hr)

Old-city core day with the wall as the main sight: enter at South Gate / Yongning Gate, rent bikes, ride west to the northwest corner or near Yuxiang Gate and descend to Guangren Temple; if bikes can't be returned at the northwest corner, descend at West Gate / Anding Gate and walk to Guangren Temple. On Saturday avoid climbing the wall too late; if suitable bikes aren't available, walk a section or use the wall sightseeing bus. The Great Mosque is optional if you have energy left.

D6

(May xx) Return

  • Seat selection
  • Check-in
  • Daming Palace National Heritage Park

Xi'an Xianyang (17:15) – Hangzhou Xiaoshan (19:40)

Sights: Daming Palace National Heritage Park (2 hr)

You won't see all of Daming Palace on departure day; focus on Danfeng Gate, Imperial Road Plaza, and the Hanyuan Hall core zone. Allow time to collect luggage at the hotel and ample airport transit in the afternoon.

Tips

  • In mid-to-late May, Xi'an days are typically 25–30°C and nights about 14–18°C, with moderate rain (roughly 8–10 rainy days per month); bring sun protection, a light jacket, and compact rain gear.
  • Weekend crowds concentrate on the city wall, Qujiang, and night-tour areas—on D5, avoid climbing the wall too late.
  • Don't visit Tang Paradise only in daylight; the night views are the core experience. On costume day, work backward from the costume return time to plan your departure.
