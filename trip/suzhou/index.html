苏州周末踏春

Suzhou Spring Weekend

苏州 4 天 3 晚：拙政园狮子林、山塘平江路与古典园林踏春。

Four days in Suzhou gardens, Shantang, and Pingjiang Road.

  • 4天3晚
  • 4 days / 3 nights

行程

时间：4月xx日-4月xx日（4天3晚）

跨市交通
火车。
市内交通
打车+地铁。
住宿
悠闲居酒店，到山塘街地铁站300m，靠近山塘街和桃花坞历史文化片区。
D1D2D3D4
D1

（4月xx日）出发

杭州西(20:16) - 苏州南(21:19)

  • 预约接站

不安排游玩，入住酒店。

D2

（4月xx日）

景点：拙政园(3小时) - 狮子林(2小时) - 平江路(3小时)

  • 拙政园
  • 狮子林

10点左右从酒店出发，打车约20分钟到拙政园，然后步行约10分钟去狮子林。午餐大概率在拙政园和狮子林一带解决。之后步行约15-20分钟转入平江路，晚餐安排在平江路，可顺带坐船、听评弹，再回酒店。

D3

（4月xx日）

景点：重元寺(5小时)

  • 重元寺

10点左右从酒店出发，打车到重元寺，就地午餐游玩。晚餐后打车去山塘街附近，登老阊门城墙，最后步行约20分钟回酒店。

D4

（4月xx日）返程

苏州(18:31) - 杭州西(20:36)

景点：留园(3小时) - 山塘街(1.5小时)

  • 留园

早上退房并将行李寄存在酒店，10点左右出发，打车或步行到留园，非常近。午餐可在留园周边或酒店附近解决，餐后还有时间去山塘街。16:30前回到酒店，取完行李出发，乘地铁去苏州站，返程。

Itinerary

Dates: Apr xx–Apr xx (4 days, 3 nights)

Intercity transport
Train.
Local transport
Taxi + metro.
Accommodation
Youxianju Hotel, 300 m from Shantang Street metro station, close to Shantang Street and the Taohuawu historic cultural district.
D1D2D3D4
D1

(Apr xx) Departure

Hangzhou West (20:16) - Suzhou South (21:19)

  • Book station pickup

No sightseeing—check into the hotel.

D2

(Apr xx)

Sights: Humble Administrator's Garden (3 hours) - Lion Grove Garden (2 hours) - Pingjiang Road (3 hours)

  • Humble Administrator's Garden
  • Lion Grove Garden

Leave the hotel around 10:00 and take a taxi (~20 minutes) to Humble Administrator's Garden, then walk ~10 minutes to Lion Grove Garden. Lunch will most likely be near the two gardens. Afterward, walk ~15–20 minutes to Pingjiang Road. Dinner on Pingjiang Road—you can also take a boat ride or listen to pingtan—then return to the hotel.

D3

(Apr xx)

Sights: Chongyuan Temple (5 hours)

  • Chongyuan Temple

Leave the hotel around 10:00 and take a taxi to Chongyuan Temple. Lunch and sightseeing on site. After dinner, taxi to the Shantang Street area, climb the old Changmen city wall, then walk ~20 minutes back to the hotel.

D4

(Apr xx) Return

Suzhou (18:31) - Hangzhou West (20:36)

Sights: Lingering Garden (3 hours) - Shantang Street (1.5 hours)

  • Lingering Garden

Check out in the morning and leave luggage at the hotel. Depart around 10:00—taxi or walk to Lingering Garden; it's very close. Lunch near Lingering Garden or the hotel. After lunch there's still time for Shantang Street. Return to the hotel before 16:30, collect luggage, take the metro to Suzhou Station, and head home.
