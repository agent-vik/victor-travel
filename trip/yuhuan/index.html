台州玉环玩海休闲

Yuhuan Seaside Getaway

台州玉环玩海度假 3 天 3 晚，高铁往返，后沙沙滩为主。

Three-day seaside vacation in Yuhuan by high-speed rail, centered on Housha beach.

  • 3 days / 3 nights
