温州楠溪江山水漫游

Yongjia Nanxi River Mountain Escape

温州永嘉楠溪江游览模式 3 天 3 晚，高铁往返，岩头镇为基地。

Three-day sightseeing trip to Yongjia’s Nanxi River by high-speed rail, based in Yantou.

  • 3天3晚
  • 3 days / 3 nights

行程

时间：9月xx日-9月xx日（3天3晚）

高铁往返。去程 杭州西18:13→楠溪江19:37 / 回程 楠溪江17:33→杭州西19:11。

打车+步行。

D0

（9月xx日）出发

杭州西(18:13) - 楠溪江站(19:37)

晚上抵达，不安排游玩。

D1

（9月xx日）

景点：石桅岩(5小时) - 丽水街(1小时)

  • 石桅岩
  • 丽水街

打车到石桅岩，午饭就近农家；傍晚打车或公交回酒店附近，可以逛逛丽水街。

D2

（9月xx日）

景点：百丈瀑(1.5小时) - 永嘉书院(4小时)

  • 百丈瀑
  • 永嘉书院

打车到百丈瀑，附近午餐选择不多，如来得及可以去下一站就餐；再打车去永嘉书院；结束打车回酒店。

D3

（9月xx日）返程

楠溪江站(17:33) - 杭州西(19:11)

景点：楠溪江竹筏漂流(2小时) - 芙蓉古村(2小时)

  • 楠溪江竹筏漂流
  • 芙蓉古村

从二桥乘竹筏至狮子岩，午饭在狮子岩一带解决；打车回芙蓉古村，来不及可跳过；回酒店拿行李，16:00打车出发去火车站。

检查清单

行李清单可使用Excel表格管理。

小提示

  • 9月底河谷仍偏热、湿度高；竹筏等水上项目注意防晒与救生。
  • 石桅岩回岩头公交不便。主要还靠打车，别太晚离场。如果只剩公交，先到鹤盛再尝试打车或公交，鹤盛到岩头公交班次也不多。

Itinerary

Dates: Sep xx–Sep xx (3 days, 3 nights)

Intercity transport
High-speed rail round trip. Outbound Hangzhou West 18:13→Nanxi River 19:37 / Return Nanxi River 17:33→Hangzhou West 19:11.
Local transport
Taxi + walking.
Accommodation
Guesthouse near Yantou Town, Yongjia.
D0D1D2D3
D0

(Sep xx) Departure

Hangzhou West (18:13) - Nanxi River Station (19:37)

Arrive in the evening; no sightseeing planned.

D1

(Sep xx)

Sights: Shiwei Rock (5h) - Lishui Street (1h)

  • Shiwei Rock
  • Lishui Street

Taxi to Shiwei Rock; lunch at a nearby farmhouse. In the evening, taxi or bus back near the hotel; stroll Lishui Street if you like.

D2

(Sep xx)

Sights: Baizhang Waterfall (1.5h) - Yongjia Academy (4h)

  • Baizhang Waterfall
  • Yongjia Academy

Taxi to Baizhang Waterfall; few lunch options nearby—eat at the next stop if time allows. Then taxi to Yongjia Academy; taxi back to the hotel afterward.

D3

(Sep xx) Return

Nanxi River Station (17:33) - Hangzhou West (19:11)

Sights: Nanxi bamboo raft (2h) - Furong Ancient Village (2h)

  • Nanxi bamboo raft
  • Furong Ancient Village

Raft from Erqiao to Lion Rock; lunch near Lion Rock. Taxi to Furong Ancient Village (skip if short on time); pick up luggage at the hotel and leave for the station by taxi at 16:00.

Tips

  • Late September valleys stay hot and humid; use sunscreen and life jackets for rafting and other water activities.
  • Buses from Shiwei Rock back to Yantou are inconvenient—rely on taxis and don’t leave too late. If only buses remain, go to Hesheng first, then try taxi or bus onward; Hesheng–Yantou buses are also infrequent.
