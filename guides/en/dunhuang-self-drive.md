## Itinerary

**Dates: Apr xx–Apr xx (5 days, 4 nights)**  
Transport: Self-drive. Dunhuang Mogao Airport is about 20 minutes from downtown; pick up and return the car at the airport.  
Accommodation: Stay in downtown Dunhuang the whole trip—Dunhuang Bohui Wenhua Hotel, 200 m from Shazhou Night Market.

### D1 Departure

Xiaoshan Airport (06:35) - Mogao Airport (12:30)

**Sights: Dunhuang Museum (2 hours)**

- [x] Dunhuang Museum reservation

Rent a car at the airport, drop luggage at the hotel, lunch near the hotel, museum in the afternoon. If time allows, see White Horse Pagoda near the museum, then wander downtown. Dinner at Shazhou Night Market. Also buy snacks for the car—the west route is barren and food is hard to find the next day.

### D2

**Sights: Western Thousand Buddha Caves (1 hour) - Yumen Pass (2 hours) - Yangguan (2 hours) - Dunhuang Film City (1 hour)**

- [ ] Western Thousand Buddha Caves tickets
- [x] Yumen Pass tickets
- [x] Yangguan tickets
- [ ] Dunhuang Film City tickets

Full day on the west route. Western Thousand Buddha Caves first, then straight to farthest Yumen Pass; lunch at the Yumen Pass visitor center. Backtrack to Yangguan; if time remains, Film City. Film City is mainly costume rentals and photos—if you’d rather skip it, stay at Yangguan for sunset, then return downtown for dinner.

### D3

**Sights: Mogao Caves (4 hours) - Again Dunhuang show (1.5 hours)**

- [x] Tickets & show tickets

Park at the visitor-center lot. At 11:30 a.m. watch two short films in the theater, then take the park shuttle to the cave zone. Our Type A tickets include a guide for 8 random caves; afterward you’re free to roam the site. At 8:00 p.m., the show is at the Mogao Caves visitor center.

### D4

**Sights: Leiyin Temple (1 hour) - Mingsha Mountain & Crescent Lake (4 hours)**

- [x] Mingsha Mountain & Crescent Lake tickets

Park at the scenic-area lot. Morning: Leiyin Temple, then lunch across the way in Crescent Lake Town. Afternoon: Mingsha Mountain & Crescent Lake, timing flexible. You can leave early, or stay for sunset and the stars.

### D5 Return

No sightseeing—go straight to the airport.

Mogao Airport (12:25) - Zhongchuan Airport (14:30)  
Zhongchuan Airport (17:50) - Xiaoshan Airport (20:40)

## Tips

- Dunhuang is 1.5 hours behind Beijing time; spring sunset is around 8:00 p.m.
- Elevation is about 1,200 m—no need to worry about altitude sickness.
- Watch the speed limits (reportedly 40–80 km/h); it’s easy to speed—keep navigation on the whole way.
- All scenic-area parking is free.
- No gas stations along the west route (toward Yumen Pass and Yardang).
- ~~The west route is said to have terrible signal and you need cash.~~ (Field-checked: not true.)
- Unlimited entries to Mingsha Mountain within three days. On first entry, scan your face on the machine by the gate for a portrait capture.
- Mogao has a Digital Dunhuang site with cave detail: [https://www.e-dunhuang.com/index.htm](https://www.e-dunhuang.com/index.htm), plus the documentary *The Complete Beauty*: [https://v.qq.com/x/page/c052998o5ua.html](https://v.qq.com/x/page/c052998o5ua.html).
- For *Again Dunhuang*, you stand and move with the story for about the first hour—not seated the whole time. No water or food allowed.
- WeChat mini program “博物官” (Bowuguan) is the Dunhuang Museum’s in-house audio guide covering many collection pieces—worth a listen if interested.
