## Tips

- During Spring Festival, Zhuhai temperatures run 15–20°C—light autumn clothing works well.
- For Macau light rail tickets, use the staffed counter to pay with WeChat or Alipay.
- For Macau taxis, use Amap (Gaode)—no worries about payment or exchange rates.
- Macau buses accept WeChat and Alipay; claim a bus ride code first.
- On Macau buses and light rail, children under 1 m ride free. Over 1 m: full fare on buses; half fare on light rail for ages under 12.
- In Macau, prefer WeChat and Alipay. When small shops don't accept them, RMB cash is often taken at 1:1 against MOP—you lose on the exchange rate.
- Macau uses UK Type G plugs—bring a compatible adapter.

**Inside Ocean Kingdom**

Animal exhibits and rides are fully mixed together. Polar Adventure and Haishen Mountain are the densest animal zones.

Timed shows fall into two groups: theaters and cinemas scattered across zones; parades and fireworks all center on Hengqin Lake. Check show times in the Chimelong Tourism app a day or two ahead when planning. The app also shows current queue times for rides.

- [ ] Aquatic Flyer Grand Show (once daily)
- [ ] Ocean Night Glow Parade (once daily)
- [ ] Spring Festival Ocean Grand Parade (once daily)
- [ ] Mammoth Grand Parade (once daily)
