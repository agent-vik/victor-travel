Hangzhou Xiaoshan (14:35) – Jinghong Gasa (18:35)

- [ ] Check-in

### D2 (Mar xx)

**Sights:** Xishuangbanna Prime Forest Park (4 hr) – Gaozhuang Xishuangjing (2 hr)

- [x] Xishuangbanna Prime Forest Park

Taxi to Prime Forest Park; eat inside the park or nearby. Afterward, taxi to Gaozhuang—visit the Great Golden Pagoda and stroll the Starlight Night Market. If taxis are hard to find, a 30-minute bus ride also works. Dinner in Gaozhuang, then taxi back to the hotel.

### D3 (Mar xx)

**Sights:** Mandiu Ancient Village (1.5 hr) – Boruo Temple (0.5 hr) – Manting Park (2 hr) – General Buddhist Temple (1 hr)

- [x] Chartered car
- [ ] Manting Park

Taxi to Mandiu Ancient Village for a walk through its authentic atmosphere; lunch in the village. Taxi to Boruo Temple for a photo stop—skip if time is tight. Then taxi to Manting Park; with your ticket you can continue into the General Buddhist Temple. Dinner nearby or around the hotel—the hotel is only about 1 km away, walkable.

### D4 (Mar xx)

**Sights:** Wild Elephant Valley (full day)

- [x] Chartered car
- [x] Wild Elephant Valley
- [x] Rainforest Elephant Trek

Chartered car to Wild Elephant Valley; lunch inside the park; full day on site. The park offers an "elephant meal" experience—dining near elephants, mostly elephant-friendly food; may require advance booking. Dinner near the hotel afterward.

Arrive at the Elephant Science Park check-in by 11:20 for Rainforest Elephant Trek.  
The south gate may not allow parking—aim to arrive at the park an hour earlier.  
Rainforest Elephant Trek tickets are separate from park admission; collect both.  
If it has rained, buy rain boots inside the park.

### D5 (Mar xx)

**Sights:** Tropical Flower Garden (3 hr) – Lancang River Riverside Walk (2 hr)

- [ ] Tropical Flower Garden

Brunch near the hotel, then taxi to Tropical Flower Garden. Afterward, taxi to the Lancang River Riverside Walk—play in the sand at White Elephant Beach; dinner nearby. Taxi back to the hotel.

- [ ] Check-in

After checkout, citywalk around the municipal government area. Taxi back to the hotel for luggage by 14:00, then taxi to the airport.

## Tips

- Xishuangbanna has four peak seasons: Spring Festival, Water Splashing Festival, summer vacation, and National Day. The period between Spring Festival and Water Splashing Festival is a clear low season.
- Local time in Xishuangbanna is about one hour behind Beijing time.
- Late March temperatures run 20–35°C; summer clothes plus a sun shirt are enough.
- Mosquitoes start appearing in late March; in-town mosquito numbers are still low.
