## Tips

- Late September valleys stay hot and humid; use sunscreen and life jackets for rafting and other water activities.
- Buses from Shiwei Rock back to Yantou are inconvenient—rely on taxis and don’t leave too late. If only buses remain, go to Hesheng first, then try taxi or bus onward; Hesheng–Yantou buses are also infrequent.
