## Itinerary

**Dates: Oct xx–Nov xx (3 days, 3 nights)**  
Intercity transport: High-speed rail.  
Local transport: Metro + taxi.  
Accommodation: Hotel near Daxinggong metro station.

### D0 (Oct xx, Thu) Departure

Hangzhou West (19:22) - Nanjing South (20:32)

Arrive in the evening, take Metro Line 3 to Daxinggong station, check in—no sightseeing planned.

### D1 (Oct xx, Fri)

**Sights: Hongshan Forest Zoo (5 hours) - Xuanwu Lake (1 hour)**

- [x] Hongshan Forest Zoo

Take Metro Line 3 to Nanjing railway station and enter the zoo through the south gate. See the entire new zone by the south gate, then have lunch at the food court. Stroll south along the eastern side of Fangniu Hill, passing Gondwana, and reach the hippo house. From the hippo house, take the shuttle to the elephant house station and explore the north side of Fangniu Hill—the Gaoligong area, koala house, and more. Walk to the cat cluster on the northeast side of Dahongshan Hill, skip the amusement area, and loop back to the new tiger enclosure. Then continue along the south of Dahongshan Hill to the dense animal zone in the southwest, taking in the Xiaohongshan area and the panda house on the way, and exit through the north gate. Fair warning: there is no way to see everything along this route—not even a third of it—so pick and choose. Xuanwu Lake is flexible; cut it if time runs short. If there's time, take the metro to Jiming Temple station, walk along the lake, and climb the Taicheng city wall if interested. For dinner, improvise along the way; if skipping Xuanwu Lake, head back to the Xinjieko downtown area to eat.

### D2 (Oct xx, Sat)

**Sights: Meiling Palace (1.5 hours) - Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (2.5 hours) - Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall (0.5 hours) - Music Stage (1 hour) - 1912 District (0.5 hours)**

- [ ] Meiling Palace
- [x] Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum
- [ ] Music Stage

Take Metro Line 2 to Muxuyuan station and have lunch in the commercial area by the station. There is a sightseeing shuttle stop right by the station—ride to Meiling Palace if available, or walk there. The stretch out of Meiling Palace is long; take the shuttle to Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum. Climb the 392 steps at a leisurely pace, then visit the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall (last entry 16:00). Afterwards, walk over to the Music Stage—buy bird feed and hand-feed the pigeons. The Music Stage is open until 18:30, but the shuttle out of the scenic area stops running earlier; watch the clock and don't linger too long, otherwise it's at least a half-hour walk out. Take the shuttle to any metro station nearby, ride Line 2 back to Daxinggong, and stroll the 1912 Republic-era district in the evening; dinner around there.

### D3 (Nov xx, Sun) Return

Nanjing South (18:01) - Hangzhou West (19:19)

**Sights: Presidential Palace (2.5 hours) - Meiyuan New Village (1 hour) - Pilu Temple (0.5 hours)**

- [x] Presidential Palace
- [ ] Pilu Temple

Walk to the Presidential Palace; have a late-morning brunch nearby before going in. Meiyuan New Village and Pilu Temple are both flexible—visit whichever you have time for. From the Presidential Palace, walk straight back to the hotel; from Meiyuan or Pilu Temple, take a taxi back. Pick up your luggage by 16:00 and take Metro Line 3 from Daxinggong straight to Nanjing South railway station.

## Tips

- Nanjing in late Oct–early Nov: daytime 19–21°C, mornings and evenings 10–12°C. Crisp autumn weather with little rain; bring a jacket for mornings and evenings.
- In-park shuttle at Hongshan Zoo (north gate ⇄ hippo house loop): outbound runs non-stop from the north gate to the hippo house; the return stops at the elephant house, monkey hill and naked mole-rat house before reaching the north gate. 15 CNY one way / 20 CNY round trip on weekdays.
- Several metro stations south of Purple Mountain have sightseeing shuttle stops that reach deep into the scenic area and connect all the main sights. Last departures vary, around 17:30—plan accordingly.
- Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum requires real-name reservation (free, official WeChat mini program, 7 days in advance, tickets released daily at 16:00); weekend slots are tight.
- The Purple Mountain combo ticket (100 CNY) covers Ming Xiaoling + Linggu + Music Stage + Meiling Palace; if you only visit the Music Stage (10 CNY) and Meiling Palace (30 CNY), buying separately totals 40 CNY—no need for the combo.
- The Presidential Palace requires real-name ticket reservation (official WeChat mini program, 2 days in advance excluding the same day, tickets released daily at 00:00); for weekend visits, be ready to grab tickets right at release time.
