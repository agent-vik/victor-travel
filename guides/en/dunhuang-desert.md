## Itinerary

**Dates: Sep xx–Sep xx (5 days, 4 nights)**  
Transport: Intercity travel—direct flight out, connecting flight back. Local travel—rent a car for 1 day on the west route; taxis elsewhere.  
Accommodation: Bohui Wenhua Hotel, near the Dunhuang night-market area.

### D1 Departure

Xiaoshan Airport (13:40) - Dunhuang Airport (17:50)

- [ ] Free hotel airport pickup

Arrive in the evening; no sightseeing. Buy food that night for the next day’s lunch—there’s little to eat on the west route.

### D2

**Sights: Western Thousand Buddha Caves (0.5 hours) - Yardang Geological Park (3.5 hours)**

- [ ] Western Thousand Buddha Caves
- [x] Yardang Geological Park

West route. Actual playtime is short; it looks roomy, but road time is long—Yardang to hotel is 2.5 hours one way alone. Finish breakfast at the hotel before 9:30 and wait for the rental company to deliver the car. If you leave after 10:30, skip the Western Thousand Buddha Caves. Yardang has two parts: a regular bus for the standard sights; you can pay extra for an off-road deep tour into the desert and more offbeat spots. Sunset at Yardang is very late—with kids, don’t count on catching it. Leave the park by 17:30, be back at the hotel by 20:30 for the rental company to pick up the car; if you arrive early, ask whether they can come sooner. Eat near the hotel.

### D3

**Sights: Mingsha Mountain (8 hours)**

- [x] Mingsha Mountain

Full day at Mingsha Mountain; taxi round-trip; schedule as you like.

### D4

**Sights: Mingsha Mountain (8 hours)**

- [x] Mingsha Mountain

Full day at Mingsha Mountain; taxi round-trip; schedule as you like.

### D5 Return

Dunhuang Airport (13:30) - Xiaoshan Airport (18:55)

- [ ] Ship luggage to the airport ahead of time

No sightseeing. Browse streets or a mall, eat early, then head home.

## Tips

- Nights can drop to the low teens (°C); daytime can near 30°C.
- Every sight is fiercely sunny—pack strong sun protection.
- The only place to eat on the west route is at the Yangguan scenic-area entrance.
